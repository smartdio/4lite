A REMEMBERED CAMPUS

Sì Xiǎo · 4Lite

This browser-based campus experience returns to a Guangdong primary school in the early 1980s. Walk through the teaching building, playground, old classrooms and shade; open doors and windows, sit at a desk, draw on a blackboard, throw chalk, shoot baskets or play table tennis.

The campus is reconstructed from first-hand memory, hand-drawn plans, historical photographs and dimensional estimates. It is a work of memory, not a measured survey; unverified dimensions and details remain working values or interpretations.

MADE BY A PERSON AND AI

A person remembers; AI helps memory become space

The developer supplies first-hand memories, historical material, spatial intuition and the repeated verdict of whether each build feels right. AI agents help organise those clues into plans, models, materials, code and tests. Every version returns to a real browser, where a person looks, walks and corrects it again.

This campus was not generated from a single picture. It has gradually emerged through sketches, discussion, studies, modelling, play and revision.

PRODUCTION AND RESOURCES

What this memory is made with

Creative and collaboration tools

Hermes supports research; Concept Draw on iPad is used for sketches; Blender for 3D work; ocenaudio for audio editing; and MiniMax Music for AI-assisted music. AI agents help with research organisation, implementation and verification.

Runtime

Three.js builds the browser-based 3D scene and interactions; Vite handles development and production builds. Match graphics use TakWolf’s Fusion Pixel Font under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Sound and music

Kenney interface, RPG and impact sounds are used under CC0. Cicadas by BlenderTimer and frogs by Kuzu420 are used under the Pixabay Content License. “Afternoon in the Schoolyard” was created by the developer with MiniMax Music and edited in ocenaudio.

3D assets

The basketball comes from a CC0 asset by DigitalN8m4r3. The table-tennis paddle by Kin Chen comes from BlenderKit, is substantially modified and remains under the BlenderKit Royalty Free licence; it is not distributed as a standalone model.
